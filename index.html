Welcome to Mellow Cellos: Come and Swing with Us!

We sell high-quality affordable cellos for aspiring and professional musicians across North Carolina. Come and stop by! We do offer low discounts for military members.

Our Services

    Cello Sales

    We offer a wide selection of new and used cellos from America, Germany, Britain, and Italy. Our cellos are made of the finest wood perfect for projecting sound, and our bows are made out of lightweight and flexible materials.

    Cello Rentals

    We provide convenient cello rental options for musicians who need a cello for a short period of time. Quarter and half size range from about $10-$30 per month. Three-quarters range at about $35 per month. Full-size cellos reach about $50 per month. We also offer a 25% discount for military members on all rentals.

    Cello Repairs

    We offer cello repair services for musicians who need to fix their instruments. Our experienced technicians can handle everything from repairs to major restorations. We also offer a 10% discount for military members on all repairs.
