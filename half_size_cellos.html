1/2 Size Cellos

We offer a wide selection of 1/2 size cellos with the best high-quality materials. Our cellos are made of the finest wood perfect for projecting sound, and our bows are made out of lightweight and flexible materials.

Most of our cellos are made of both spruce and maple. Spruce is used for the front plate, while maple is used for the back and sides. While other woods such as poplar and willow are also used, spruce and maple are the most common.
